New PAN card

What to keep ready, what it costs, and which official site to use. Saathi shows the steps before you start. You finish on the official site yourself.

What to keep ready

  • ID proof: Aadhaar, voter ID, passport or driving licence
  • Address proof: Aadhaar, voter ID, passport, driving licence, or a recent electricity bill or bank statement
  • Date-of-birth proof: birth certificate, school leaving or Class 10 certificate, or passport. Aadhaar is not accepted as date-of-birth proof.
  • A passport-size photo and your signature

With Aadhaar e-KYC you usually don’t need to upload papers. Since April 2026 the portal may ask for extra supporting documents, so check its list.

What it costs

  • Instant e-PAN with Aadhaar OTP: free.
  • Full application: ₹91 plus GST (about ₹107) for an Indian address, paid on the portal.
  • Correction or reprint: about ₹50 with GST for an Indian address. The portal shows the exact fee before you pay.

Official sites

  • Income Tax Department: incometax.gov.in
  • Protean e-Gov (PAN): tinpan.proteantech.in
  • UTIITSL (PAN): pan.utiitsl.com

Keep your acknowledgement number safe until the card arrives.

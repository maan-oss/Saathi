Driving licence renewal or duplicate

What to keep ready to renew your licence or get a duplicate, and what it costs. You finish on Sarathi, the official site.

What to keep ready

  • Your existing driving licence number and date of birth
  • Form 2, filled online on Sarathi
  • Form 1: self-declaration of physical fitness
  • Form 1A: medical certificate from a registered doctor. Needed for transport vehicles, and some official pages also ask for it over age 40.
  • Passport-size photo and signature

For a duplicate: Form 2 plus the damaged licence, or a photocopy or FIR if it was lost. Your state may ask for more.

What it costs

  • Central fee list: renewal ₹200. Late renewal ₹300, plus ₹1,000 for each year or part year after the first.
  • Adding a vehicle class: ₹500.
  • States add their own user charges, smart-card and postage costs. The payment screen shows the final amount, and the duplicate fee appears there too.

Official sites

  • Sarathi (Parivahan): sarathi.parivahan.gov.in
  • Parivahan: parivahan.gov.in

    Questions

    Straight answers.

    Is Saathi an official government service?

    No. Saathi is an independent helper and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any government body. It always points you to the official website, and rules and fees can change, so confirm on the official site before you pay.

    Does it log in to government sites for me?

    No, and it never will. Your OTP, captcha and consent belong to you. Saathi prepares your details and explains each step; you do the final steps yourself.

    What happens to my details and my locker?

    Nothing is saved unless you choose to. If you do, your details and your locker (ID numbers and expiry dates) are encrypted on our server, hidden until you tap Unlock, and erased any time by typing “forget”. Photos are read once and never kept. There are no ads. Read the full notice on the privacy page.

    Can Saathi read my documents from a photo?

    Yes, if you allow it. A photo or PDF is read by an AI model to pull out your name, date of birth, address, ID number and expiry date. You see what it found and choose Save, Use once, or Discard. The photo itself is never kept.

    Do I need WhatsApp?

    No. The web app works in any browser and can be added to your home screen. If you do use WhatsApp, it is the same Saathi there.

    How much does it cost?

    New people get a small welcome credit and some free messages every day. After that, each action has a small price, shown above when prices are set. There is no subscription.

    What if I lose my phone or clear my browser?

    In the web app, open Settings and save your backup code. Paste it on a new device to get your balance back.

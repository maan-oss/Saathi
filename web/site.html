    Built to keep you safe.

    Saathi is an independent helper. These are the rules it works by.

    Saathi does

    • Shows the documents, the fee and the official page before you start.
    • Keeps your locker encrypted. ID numbers and expiry dates stay hidden until you unlock them.
    • Checks a link or message for signs of a scam.
    • Explains each step in English or Hindi.

    Saathi never

    • Asks for an OTP, PIN, password or card number.
    • Logs in to a government site for you, or fills in a captcha.
    • Pays a government fee on your behalf.

    Pay only for what you use.

    Your balance is in Wallet in the app. Each action takes a small amount from it.

    Strange message? Check it first.

    Paste a link, SMS or offer. Nothing you paste is saved, and no link is opened.

    Never paste an OTP or card number.

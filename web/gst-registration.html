GST registration

What to keep ready to register your business for GST. The GST portal does not charge a registration fee. If someone asks you to pay to “process” it, that is an agent’s fee, not the government’s.

What to keep ready

  • PAN of the business or the owner
  • Proof of how the business is set up: partnership deed or incorporation certificate (not needed for a sole proprietor)
  • Owner or partner details: PAN, Aadhaar, address and photo. Companies also need the DIN.
  • Proof for the authorised signatory: appointment letter or board resolution, and a photo
  • Proof of the business place: ownership document, a rent agreement with the owner’s consent, or a utility bill
  • Bank details: a cancelled cheque or the first page of a statement. You can add these later.
  • A mobile number linked to Aadhaar and a working email for the OTPs

Some states ask for extra items, such as an electricity consumer number.

What it costs

  • No registration fee on the GST portal.

Official sites

  • GST portal: gst.gov.in
  • GST registration: reg.gst.gov.in

Keep your ARN until approval comes.
